# Name, logo and character

The [license](LICENSE.md) covers stepbro terminal's code. It does not cover its brand. The
following belong to stepbro lab and are not licensed for use by other projects:

- the names **stepbro** and **stepbro terminal**;
- the stepbro logo and the app and tray icons, in `resources/icons` and `build`;
- **Topito**, the mole character, and his artwork.

## What you can do

- Say that your project is based on, forked from, or works with stepbro terminal, by name, as long
  as it is clear that it is not made or endorsed by stepbro lab.
- Show the logo and screenshots of the app when you write or talk about stepbro terminal itself.
- Use the name and icons unchanged in a build you make for your own use.

## What you cannot do

- Publish or share a modified build, or any other product, under the stepbro name or with its
  logo, icons or Topito.
- Use a name or icon close enough to be confused with stepbro terminal.
- Suggest that stepbro lab made, supports or endorses your project.

A fork you share needs a name and an icon of its own. Keep the license and its
`Required Notice` line with the code, as the license requires, and say where it came from.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask in
[Discussions](https://github.com/stepbro-lab/StepbroTerminal/discussions) before you publish it.
